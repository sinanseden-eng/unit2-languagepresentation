Unit 2: Language in Use

Short manga scenes about money, plans, and a school fair. Start with what the speaker means, then see why the form fits.

  1. 1Read the situation
  2. 2Connect meaning and form
  3. 3Make it your own
How to use this lesson · teacher and learner guide
  1. Choose a scene. Read its situation and predict what the characters might say.
  2. Read or play the manga. Pause on the yellow words. Use the panel numbers to move at your own pace.
  3. Make the connection. Follow the matching quote, meaning, visual guide, and form. Discuss the “Think” question before opening its answer.
  4. Use the language. Try the speaking prompt with a partner, swap roles, and compare with one possible answer.

For teachers: one scene is open in each structure. Opening another scene folds the previous one away. The grammar reference and extra slides are available when you want to extend the lesson.

Useful grammar words: a base verb is the simple form, such as buy or help. A past participle is the form used after have, such as finished, bought, or sold.
