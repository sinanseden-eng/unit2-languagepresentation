Present simpleA fact, a habit, or a fixed timetable.Read 3 manga dialogues

Use the present simple for facts and routines. It can also describe a future event when its time is part of a fixed schedule.

Form: Subject + base verb. With he, she or it, usually add -s.

Form: Subject + past verb: helped, bought, paid. Questions use did + subject + base verb.

will + base verbA decision now, a prediction, an offer, a promise, or a request.Read 5 manga dialogues

The situation tells you what will means. Read who is speaking, what has just happened, and why they are saying it.

Form: will + base verb. I'll = I will; won't = will not. A request often starts Will you…?

The Future in the Past

The future in the past means looking forward from an earlier moment. We can describe a plan, a prediction, or an arrangement that was still in the future at that time. It may have happened or it may have changed; the story gives us that information.

Read a past plan, prediction, or arrangement in context

Picture yourself at an earlier moment and look forward from there. For example, on Monday a Friday meeting was still in the future.

was / were going to + base verbA plan or prediction seen from an earlier time.Read 2 manga dialogues

Think back to a past moment. At that moment, the action was still in the future. The rest of the story tells you how things turned out.

Form: was / were + going to + base verb. Negative: wasn't / weren't going to…

Use the past continuous to describe a future arrangement when you are telling a story from a past point of view. A time clue helps make the arrangement clear.

was / were supposed to + base verbWhat someone was expected to do.Read 1 manga dialogue

Use this phrase for an earlier duty, rule, or agreed responsibility. It tells you what was expected. The rest of the sentence tells you whether it happened.

Form: was / were + supposed to + base verb

Future continuous: will be + verb-ingAn activity in progress at a future time; sometimes a guess about now.Read 3 manga dialogues

Imagine looking at an activity while it is happening. Usually you look ahead to a future time or period. In some contexts, will be + verb-ing expresses a belief about what is happening now.

Form: will + be + verb-ing. Negative: won't be + verb-ing.
