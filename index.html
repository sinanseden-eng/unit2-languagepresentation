Exploring the Future in the Past & Future Tenses

The Future in the Past

We use "was/were going to" to talk about past plans or intentions, especially when those plans did not happen. It helps us tell stories about changed decisions and unexpected outcomes.

Future Tenses with "will"

We use different tenses with "will" to make predictions about the future. The **Future Continuous** (will be + -ing) describes an action in progress at a future time, while the **Future Perfect** (will have + past participle) describes an action that will be completed by a future time.
